# Design Hub guide

Design Hub turns a folder on your Mac into a place where Claude builds prototypes, you look at them in your browser, and clients comment on a private link. This guide is for people who do not use a terminal.

Read it in order the first time. Each page is short.

1. [Set up, once](setup.md). Node.js, the Design Hub app, your first project. About fifteen minutes.
2. [Work with Claude](working.md). Asking for prototypes, opening them, using Controls.
3. [Share a link](sharing.md). The password, what reviewers see, what to send.
4. [Comments](comments.md). Invite links, who can do what, acting on feedback.
5. [When something goes wrong](help.md). The messages you may see and what to do.

You need a Mac, the Claude Code app, and, to share, a free account at vercel.com.

How the pieces fit: your project is a folder. Claude writes prototypes into it. The Design Hub app shows them in your browser, on your computer only. When you share, Claude puts the same prototypes on the web behind a password. Comments from reviewers come back into the folder, where Claude works from them. Nothing lives only in Claude; the folder is the work.
