# Comments

Two ways into a shared site. The password lets anyone look. An invite link lets one named person comment.

## Turn comments on

> Turn on comments.

The first time, Vercel asks you to accept the comment store's terms in your browser; Claude gives you the link. Claude then updates the site and gives you your own invite link. You are the first editor.

## Roles

A **reviewer** comments. An **editor** also resolves comments, sorts them and invites people. Clients are reviewers; your team are editors.

## Invite someone

On the site, open **Review**, then **People**. Enter their name, their team (your studio, or the client's company) and their role. Copy the link and send it to them. Each link is for one person. When they open it, the page says who they are invited as; they press the button and they are in.

Or say it:

> Invite Dana from Acme as a reviewer.

## Leave a comment

Open **Review**. Choose what to comment on: click the part of the page you mean, or **The whole page**. Write the comment, set its **Kind** and **State** if you like, and **Post**.

A comment remembers exactly what you were looking at, including every Controls setting. Later, **Open** on a comment brings that view back. Anyone can **Reply**. Editors **Resolve** a comment, and **Reopen** it if needed.

## Work from the comments

> What did the client say?

Claude pulls the comments into the project, sorts them by state and kind, acts on each one, resolves the ones it handled, and lists the ones that need your decision. Then say **share** again so the site shows the changes.

## Remove someone

> Revoke Dana's access.

Their link and their sessions stop. For a fresh link instead, say **Reissue Dana's link**.
